## Overview

The PCTE Training & Placement Cell platform digitizes the complete placement workflow between the **T&P Department, Students, and Recruiting Companies**.

The system allows the Training & Placement Department to publish and manage job opportunities, maintain company information, manage student applications, organize recruitment drives, and schedule guest lectures.

Students can view newly posted opportunities in real time, check eligibility and company details, apply for suitable positions, and track their application status.

The platform also provides a hierarchical administration system where a **Super Admin** can create and manage role-based Sub Admin accounts with controlled permissions.

---

## Core Features

### Student Portal

- Student authentication and account management
- View available placement opportunities
- View complete company and job details
- Check eligibility criteria before applying
- Apply for available job opportunities
- Track applied jobs and application status
- View placement-related announcements
- View upcoming guest lectures and sessions
- Access recruitment and placement information from a centralized dashboard

### Training & Placement Department

- Create and publish job opportunities
- Add complete company information
- Define job descriptions and eligibility criteria
- Manage application deadlines
- Manage recruitment drives
- View students who applied for specific opportunities
- Track student application and selection status
- Manage placement-related announcements
- Schedule and manage guest lectures
- Track upcoming and completed guest lectures
- Maintain company and recruitment information

### Guest Lecture Management

The platform also manages guest lectures and industry sessions conducted by the college.

T&P administrators can:

- Schedule guest lectures
- Add speaker/guest details
- Define date and time
- Add session information
- Track upcoming lectures
- Mark lectures as completed
- Manage lecture history
